About

The studio

The Planners is an event planning studio dedicated to occasions that feel considered, calm, and distinctly yours. From first conversation through the day of celebration, we bring structure to beauty—so hosts can stay present while every detail lands with intention.

We partner with clients who value clarity as much as creativity: thoughtful timelines, careful vendor coordination, and a presence that is polished without being impersonal. Whether you are planning a wedding, a milestone celebration, or a refined gathering, our role is to translate your vision into a seamless experience.

What we do

  • Full and partial event planning
  • Day-of coordination and on-site presence
  • Design direction, vendor liaison, and timeline management
  • Décor & rentals (overview only — no SKUs or public prices)
